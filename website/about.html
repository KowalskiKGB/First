About First

First is an independent modified version of openGym with its own Git history, source repository, deployment configuration, and public origin.

The foundation was created as openGym by Duarte Santos: a self-hosted training log built to keep workout and body-weight data under the operator's control. That upstream work supplied the workout flow, passkey profiles, PWA and mobile foundations, and the core training tools imported into this repository.

First began modifying that snapshot on 29 August 2026. The independent version is maintained at KowalskiKGB/First and is deployed at first.rocketxsistemas.com.br. It does not present itself as the original project or erase the original copyright.

The GNU AGPL remains in force. First preserves the upstream copyright notice, the app-store exception recorded by the original copyright holder, the MIT notice for third-party body-map geometry, and the separate licensing boundary for exercise media. The complete record is in NOTICE.md.

Principles

  • User-controlled data. Self-hosted profiles and portable backups avoid service lock-in.
  • Independent, not unattributed. First has its own source identity while preserving upstream authorship and notices.
  • Source-built deployment. The checked-out repository builds the two Compose services; no prebuilt image is assumed.
  • Clear licensing boundaries. Program code is AGPL, exercise data and text retain MIT terms, and exercise media needs separate permission.
  • Roadmap honesty. Student/trainer collaboration and the other unchecked roadmap modules remain future work until implemented and verified.
